Nox Armory Lite (or NX Lite for short) is a mod made mostly with Joker Forge for pouring my ideas and art for a more chaotic Balatro experience, this mod is designed with little balance in mind so the numbers can go brrrr.

# Current addition as of 1.2
* 150 Jokers
* 7 Decks
* 12 Vouchers
* 4 Booster Packs
* 7 Consumables
* 1 Enhancement
* A hidden Fusion and Upgrade mechanic
* And more upcoming...
